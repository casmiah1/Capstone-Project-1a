Recipe instructions

Avocado & Egg Toast

Ingredients: Bread, avocado, egg, lemon and black pepper.

Method: Toast the bread. Mash the avocado with lemon and pepper. Cook the egg and serve on top.

Rainbow Veggie Bowl

Ingredients: Mixed greens, tomato, cucumber, carrot, chickpeas, olive oil and lemon.

Method: Wash and chop the vegetables. Add chickpeas, olive oil and lemon juice, then mix.

Lemon Chicken & Greens

Ingredients: Chicken breast, broccoli, lemon, garlic and olive oil.

Method: Season the chicken with lemon and garlic. Cook thoroughly, prepare the greens and serve.
